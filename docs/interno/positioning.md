# The Blueprint's positioning

[Português](../pt-BR/interno/positioning.md)

Decisions made on 25 Oct 2026 (the user delegated; they can overrule any of them).

## Who it is for

People who program with a coding agent (Claude Code, Codex) and want to **control what the agent plans** and **have proof of what was done**, without handing their data to a service: solo developers, freelancers who show proposals to clients and small teams that already use agents.

## The promise

The agent proposes, you approve, and completing requires evidence. Everything local, in a small executable, with no account and no telemetry.

## Sentences (the first is the adopted one)

1. **Local-first roadmap for AI coding agents: they propose, you approve, completion needs evidence.** (95 characters, serves as the repository description)
2. A roadmap your coding agent plans in and you stay in control of.
3. In Portuguese: *O roadmap que o seu agente de IA propõe, você aprova e ele prova.*

## Language

**English first** (`README.md`) and a complete Portuguese version (`README.pt-BR.md`); the interface follows the Windows language. Reasons: all seven projects studied have an English README, the MCP and agent audience is global, and the repository must be found by English search. Cost: two versions to keep in sync, which is why a script compares the headings and commands of both.

## What not to promise

Do not promise Mac and Linux support, team collaboration or a phone app: they are in the [ROADMAP](../../ROADMAP.md) as plans, not deliveries. Say it is beta and Windows in the window.
